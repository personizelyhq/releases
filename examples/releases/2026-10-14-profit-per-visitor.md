Revenue per visitor can crown a variant that wins on sales but loses on margin, for example when a discount drives more orders. Reports now show **profit per visitor** for every variant, using the product costs already stored in Shopify.

## How to use it

- Open any experiment and switch the primary metric to **Profit per visitor**.
- Products without a cost in Shopify are counted at full price. A note in the report tells you how many orders that affects.

Read the [setup guide](https://help.personizely.net) if your costs live outside Shopify.
